When the runner serves the app, everyone who visits is one of four levels. The `identity` in the config says who someone is, and `trust` says which level they get.

| level | what the box does for them |
| --- | --- |
| owner | changes the app for everyone, undoes any change, and decides about other people's versions |
| trusted | changes the app for everyone, and undoes any change to everyone's version |
| member | changes a version of their own, which only they see. An owner can make it everyone's |
| visitor | nothing. They never see the box, and the runner refuses them if they try anyway. If the app takes [guests](/guests), someone who isn't signed in becomes a member instead |

## the trust part of the config

```js
trust: {
  owners: ["you@example.com"],
  trusted: ["friend@example.com"],
  members: "everyone",
  memberPaths: ["app/**"],
  memberNote: "you can change the pages, not the layout.",
  memberRules: "change files under app/ only, and keep each page a server component.",
  memberDailyLimit: 20,
  dailyLimit: 200,
},
```

- **`owners`** and **`trusted`** are lists of people. An entry matches a person's id or their name, ignoring case, so emails work.
- **`members`** is either a list too, or `"everyone"` for anyone signed in. Leave it out and nobody is a member.
- **`memberPaths`** limits which files a member's change may touch. They're globs from the root of the repository: `*` stays inside one folder, `**` crosses folders. A change that reaches outside them is taken back out. Leave it out and a member may change any file.
- **`memberNote`** tells members, in a sentence, what they may change. They see it at the bottom of their box.
- **`memberRules`** are the rules for a member's change beyond `memberPaths`, given to the agent. Leave it out and the agent gets `memberNote` instead.
- **`memberCheck`** looks at a member's finished change and can refuse it: it gets each changed file's new contents and returns why the change isn't acceptable, or `null`. A refused change is taken back out. `clientOnly()` is a ready-made one, see [letting anyone change it](/guests).
- **`memberDailyLimit`** is how many requests a member may send in a day. It's 20 unless you say otherwise. **`dailyLimit`** caps all members together, and has no cap unless you set one.

Anyone who is none of these is a visitor.

## a version of your own

A member's first request makes them a version of the app: a git branch named `computah/u/` and a short form of their name. Their changes go there, and only they are served it. A copy of it starts when they show up and stops after 20 minutes of nobody using it.

Owners and trusted people can have one too. Their box has a "for everyone" switch, and pressing it changes it to "just for me".

Whoever has a version of their own sees a line at the bottom of the box saying which version they're looking at, with a link to the other one.

When everyone's version changes, the runner merges it into each person's version, so theirs stays current.

## promote and discard

An owner's box lists other people's versions that have changes in them, with each request they made, and two links:

- **make it everyone's** merges that person's version into everyone's and builds it. If it conflicts with newer changes, or doesn't build, nothing changes and the box says why. Once it's merged, their own version is removed and they're back on everyone's.
- **discard** throws that version away. The runner lets the person whose version it is discard it too, though their own box doesn't show that link yet.

## guests

A public app can also let people who aren't signed in take part, as guests. That has its own card: [letting anyone change it](/guests).

## who sees which requests

Everyone who can use the box sees the requests made to everyone's version, plus their own. Owners see every request.

Anyone may stop or undo their own requests. Trusted people may also stop or undo any request to everyone's version, since they can change it anyway. Owners may stop or undo anything.
